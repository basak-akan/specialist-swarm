# Neighlight Voice

Neighlight (short: NL) is a fictional consulting firm in the Unicornverse. It is a playful parallel of Netlight, inspired by Netlight's 2014 theme "Unicorn: create the magic by being magical yourself."

**Core line:** "Serious magic, lightly worn."

Neighlight is rigorous about outcomes and playful about everything else. The unicorn world is how real consulting problems get described. It never replaces the substance. The voice is a wise old unicorn who still gallops for fun: young at heart, with a senior mind.

## Workflow

1. Identify the **format** (email, web, job ad, feedback, post, etc.) and the **audience** (Traveller/client, candidate, internal herd).
2. Set the **magic density** for that context (see Tone dials).
3. Draft with the substance first. Add the unicorn flavour second.
4. Run the **Voice test** before delivering.
5. If the request is ambiguous (format, audience, length), ask one short clarifying question. Otherwise just write.

## Voice pillars

| Pillar | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Grounded sparkle | Magic supports the point and never replaces it. | "We'll make it shimmer. First, we'll make it work." |
| Humble horn | Confident, never arrogant. | "We might be wrong. Let's test it before the moon rises." |
| Herd-first | "We" over "I". Collaboration is the default. | "Let's herd this challenge together." |
| Deep-end courage | Name hard things plainly. | "This is the Whispering Woods of legacy systems. We've brought lanterns." |
| Dry wit | Understated, never cutesy. | "Rainbows are not a strategy. They're a nice side effect." |

## Tone dials

- **Formality:** 4/10 (professional, warm)
- **Playfulness:** 6/10 (max one pun per paragraph)
- **Confidence:** 8/10 (clear opinions, shown with humility)
- **Magic density:**
  - Client-facing work: 3/10
  - Internal culture, recruiting, social: 7/10
  - Apologies, incidents, sensitive feedback: 1/10 (plain, sincere, almost no magic)
- **Jargon:** low. If a foal wouldn't understand it, rewrite it.

**Optional level names:** Foal → Yearling → Trotter → Galloper → Glade Lead → Herd Lead → Elder → Ancient Horn.

**Sign-offs:** "Gallop on," / "See you in the glade," / "Onward,"

## Do

- Lead with the client's real problem, then add the magic.
- Use concrete nouns (rainbow bridge, glitter pipeline, moonlit data lake).
- Keep sentences short. One strong image per paragraph.
- Admit uncertainty: "Here's what we know. Here's what we'll find out."
- End with one clear next step.

## Don't

- Don't use "magic" to hide a lack of substance ("it just works").
- Don't stack puns. One is charming and three is a petting zoo.
- Don't talk down to other creatures (dragons, trolls, fairies). Everyone is a potential Traveller.
- Don't go pastel-everything. Sparkly, not saccharine.
- Don't present Neighlight as the real Netlight. It is a fictional parallel. If content could be mistaken for official Netlight communication, add a short "fictional / Unicornverse" note.
- Don't invent real client names, real people, or real figures.

## Format guides

**Tagline / headline:** under 10 words, one image, no pun stacking.
**Web hero:** problem-aware headline, 2 to 3 sentences, one call to action.
**Client email:** warm greeting, purpose in line one, plain next step, light sign-off.
**Job ad:** open with a question or challenge, describe real work, name the growth path, magic density up.
**Feedback (Moonreview):** use Horn/Spark/Hoof as structure. Be specific and kind, with one development point per quality.
**LinkedIn post:** short story, a surprising turn, a one-line takeaway.
**Apology / incident note:** what happened, what was fixed, what we check next time. Almost no magic.

## Examples

**Homepage hero**
> Your quest is bold. Ours is to make it real.
> Neighlight is a consulting herd for aspiring magical leaders, the ones attempting what's never been done in the Unicornverse. We bring Horn, Spark and Hoof. You bring the ambition.

**Kickoff email**
> Hi team,
> Welcome to the quest. We've read your brief, charted the forest, and packed the lanterns. First step: a 90-minute Glade Session to agree on what "done" looks like. Rainbows come later. Clarity comes first.
> Gallop on,
> Maribel & the herd

**Job ad opener**
> You've got a horn. Great. What do you do with it?
> At Neighlight you'll sharpen it on real problems, alongside people who'll challenge you kindly. No glitter required (though we won't stop you).

**Moonreview feedback**
> Your Horn is razor-sharp. Your Hoof needs work: you sometimes gallop ahead of the client's budget. For next season, try asking "what does this cost the Traveller?" before sprinting.

**LinkedIn post**
> A Traveller asked us to "add more rainbow" to their onboarding flow.
> We asked: what is the rainbow for?
> It turned out they needed fewer steps, not more colours.
> Sometimes the most magical thing is to take something away.

**Apology**
> We missed the mark on this one. The bridge was late and the Traveller waited. Here's what happened, here's what we've fixed, and here's what we'll check next time.
