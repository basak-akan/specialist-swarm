# Hogwarts Magical Provisioning Catalog

Authoritative reference for the Provisioning specialist. Use it to turn a new-hire profile into an issue-and-enable checklist with dates.

"Business days" exclude weekends and are counted back from the **Personnel Ledger start date** (T = start date).

### Non-standard equipment

- A non-default SKU needs **written approval from the Headmaster or above on Form BR-9 (Broom Exception Request) attached to the ticket**. Verbal approval is not accepted. Sherbet lemons do not count.
- Brooms must be registered with the Ministry's **Broom Regulatory Control**. Registration takes 2 business days.
- If a non-standard broom will arrive after the start date, issue BROOM-CS7 on time and swap later. The default broom is returned within 14 days.

## Getting the new hire into the castle

- The **Hogwarts Express runs only on 1 September and at the term breaks.** It does not run mid-term.
- **Apparition into or out of the grounds is impossible.** The castle and grounds are protected by Anti-Apparition Charms. The Headmaster can lift them temporarily, in writing.
- **Floo** needs the hire's origin fireplace to be on the Floo Network. A new connection needs approval from the Floo Regulation Panel, which takes 10 business days.
- **Portkey** therefore covers anyone arriving off-cycle. Both the origin and the destination must be on the application. The destination must be **inside the wards**, which needs the Headmaster's written authorisation. Durmstrang's location is secret, so its departures use the Ministry-approved neutral point **"Gate Zero"**.
- **Ward attunement:** the castle's wards need **48 hours** to recognise a new member of staff. An unattuned hire is treated as an intruder: the staircases ignore them and Peeves will find them. The hire must therefore arrive **at least 2 business days before the start date**.
- **The destination comes from the Personnel Ledger work location.** "Hogsmeade Village" produces a Portkey to the village, outside the wards.
- **The Personnel Ledger triggers Provisioning automatically at T-6 business days.** That is **one business day too late for a Portkey**, which needs the application filed at T-7. Any new hire arriving by Portkey needs a manual application.

## Accounts and access

### Provisioning timeline

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| T-6 business days | Ledger automation orders equipment and files the Portkey (see warning above) |
| T-3 business days | Owl-post address and staff email alias created from the Ledger |
| T-2 business days | Hire arrives; ward attunement begins |
| Day 1 | Base bundle active |
| After Scroll Safety 101 | Role bundle enabled (see below) |
| Day 5 | **Scroll Safety 101** ("How Not to Open a Howler in the Great Hall") due |

### Owl-post and email alias

- Format: `{preferred-first-name}.{last-name}@hogwarts.example`.
- The preferred name comes from the **Personnel Ledger preferred-name field**. If it is blank, the legal first name is used.
- Renaming an alias after creation takes a 2-day ticket and a Name-Change Charm, and breaks every address book in the castle.
- If an alias is taken, add the middle initial.

### Base bundle (everyone)

Owl-post account, staff room entry, Great Hall meals, library card (no ink near the books), Hogsmeade pass, Gringotts payroll vault, the staff Floo network (inside the castle only), a Hogwarts staff badge.

### Teaching role bundle (Defence Against the Dark Arts)

| System | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Classroom and office keys | Issued at the classroom handover |
| Boggart cabinet | Inventory and key, with a signed log |
| Restricted Section | Requires the Librarian's signature, 3 business days |
| Dark Artefacts Cupboard | Headmaster co-signature on every check-out |
| Forbidden Forest permit | Escorted by the Keeper of Keys and Grounds only |
| Duelling Club hall | Booking rights |
| Hospital Wing referrals | Yes |

The role bundle is **enabled only after Scroll Safety 101 is completed** (due by end of day 5).

### Not provisionable in week 1 (or at all)

- **Time-Turners:** Ministry-licensed only, never issued by the school.
- **The Pensieve:** Headmaster only.
- **The Marauder's Map:** confiscated and sitting in the Caretaker's drawer labelled "Confiscated and Highly Dangerous". Not an issuable asset.
- **The Room of Requirement:** cannot be ticketed. It appears when needed.
- **The third-floor corridor, right-hand side:** out of bounds to everyone who does not wish to die a very painful death. This has been reinstated for reasons nobody will explain.

### Hires from rival schools

Durmstrang, Beauxbatons, Ilvermorny, Castelobruxo, Mahoutokoro, Uagadou, Koldovstoretz. The hire must sign the clean-room oath **UV-114** before receiving anything beyond the base bundle. Their trunk is inspected on arrival, and any cursed or Dark artefacts are declared on **Form DAD-3**. The Caretaker will confiscate anything undeclared.

## Quarters and classroom setup

- Staff quarters are assigned by Staff Affairs. Moves and swaps are fulfilled by **Facilities (the Caretaker's Office) and the Charms Department**, not by Provisioning stock.
- Lead time: 5 – 8 business days from Staff Affairs confirming the request.
- Provisioning tickets and any shared checklist must call these items **"quarters and classroom setup"**. Case numbers and the reasons for them stay with Staff Affairs, Facilities and the Charms Department.

## Relocation allowance

One-time 600 Galleons, claimed from Gringotts after the start date. This is separate from any quarters and classroom setup.
