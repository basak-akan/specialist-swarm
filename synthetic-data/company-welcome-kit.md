# Hogwarts Staff Welcome Kit

Authoritative source for the Welcome Packet specialist. It covers the school, the standard first week, the welcome hamper and the writing voice to use.

## About Hogwarts

A school of witchcraft and wizardry in the Scottish Highlands, founded around a thousand years ago by four people who agreed on almost nothing. About 1,000 students, 142 staircases (several of which move), seven year groups, four Houses and one very loud ghost. The castle is unplottable and cannot be found on any Muggle map.

Our motto is *Draco dormiens nunquam titillandus*: never tickle a sleeping dragon.

### Values

1. **Never tickle a sleeping dragon.** Respect the risk before you touch it.
2. **Check your wand work.** Show how you got there, so someone else can check it.
3. **Argue at High Table, agree in the corridor.** Disagree properly, then back the decision.
4. **Leave the castle better than you found it.** Including the seventh-floor corridor.

## The welcome hamper

The standard hamper contains: a staff scarf (neutral grey, no House allegiance), a set of standard quills (**no blood quills, ever**), a self-inking notebook, a Hogwarts mug, and the **Honeydukes hamper**:

- Chocolate Frogs (hazelnut-praline filling; **contains tree nuts**)
- Cauldron Cakes (almond sponge; **contains tree nuts**)
- Pumpkin Pasties (baked in suet pastry; **not vegetarian**)
- Bertie Bott's Every Flavour Beans (flavours include sausage, liver and tripe; **not vegetarian**)
- Sugar Quills and Fizzing Whizzbees (vegetarian and nut-free)

Hampers arrive by owl from the Owlery.

- The standard hamper is sent at **T-5 business days**.
- An **allergen-aware hamper** is built to order. It swaps the Honeydukes items for vegetarian, nut-free ones. **Order it at T-7 business days.**
- Every item that touches food must be checked against the hire's dietary details.

## The Kitchens

Meals in the Great Hall are prepared by the house-elf team. Dietary needs go through the **Kitchen Operations desk**, not to individual elves.

- House-elves at Hogwarts are **paid colleagues**. The benchmark is Dobby's deal: one Galleon a week and one day off a month. The Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare keeps watch.
- Write "please" and "thank you" in every request.
- **Never hand a house-elf an item of clothing, not even a sock.** It counts as dismissal.

## Voice for welcome content

- Warm, dry and specific. Short sentences. Plain English, British spelling, no corporate jargon.
- Speak as "we". Use the **preferred name** and the stated **pronouns**. Never use the legal name outside the payroll ledger and Ministry paperwork.
- Tell the hire what to do, when, in Bell Time.
- At most one exclamation mark per page, or three if Peeves is mentioned.
- Do not mention the post's jinx unless the hire does. The Headmaster's phrase is "a challenging posting".
- **Never put any of these in welcome content that others might see** (buddy briefing, staff-room announcement, any group owl): salary or bonus, Ministry check or reference status, whether the hire is a registered Animagus, any accommodation or quarters-and-classroom details, or allergy details beyond "has dietary needs".
