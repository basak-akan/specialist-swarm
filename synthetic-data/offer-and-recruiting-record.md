## 4. Rules for starting (Board of Governors Appointment Policy)

1. **Ministry clearance must be Clear before the start date. No exceptions.** "Clear" means every row in the checks table above is closed: criminal, qualifications, Veritaserum interview, Wand Registry, Animagus Registry, and every employment verification.
2. **Animagus Registry:** every Animagus must be registered with the Ministry's Improper Use of Magic Office. An unregistered Animagus is liable to imprisonment in Azkaban. If a candidate discloses an unregistered form during hiring, they must **self-report and register before the start date**. Board policy is that voluntary self-registration is processed in 5 business days and not referred to the Aurors.
3. **Alternative verification for a dissolved employer:** any two of (a) a Ministry Tax Scroll, (b) a Gringotts payslip or vault statement, (c) a signed statement from a former supervisor. **Ghosts cannot sign documents** (Board Resolution 1412), so a ghost's testimony is admissible only as a Pensieve memory, which needs the Headmaster's written consent and takes about 10 business days. Staff Affairs approves within 2 business days of receiving the second document.
4. **Owl references:** three are required. A candidate may start with fewer if the Headmaster signs a waiver on **Form REC-22 (Owl Reference Waiver)**. The missing reference must be completed within 14 days of the start date, or the offer is subject to review.
5. **Work permit:** foreign hires need a Ministry work permit (Form IMC-9¾). Part 1 is completed by the end of day 1. Part 2 is counter-signed in person at the Department of International Magical Cooperation within 3 business days of the start date. Booking the Ministry appointment takes 2 business days.
6. **Sign-on bonus:** must be entered in the Personnel Ledger at least 5 business days before the start date, or it misses the first Gringotts payroll run.
7. **Hires from a rival school** (Durmstrang, Beauxbatons, Ilvermorny, Castelobruxo, Mahoutokoro, Uagadou, Koldovstoretz) must sign the clean-room oath **Form UV-114** by day 1. UV-114 is an ordinary witnessed oath. **Unbreakable Vows are banned in employment contracts.**
8. **Rider 14b** must appear on every offer for the Defence Against the Dark Arts post. It sets a one-year term and schedules a wellbeing check at St Mungo's at the end of term.
9. **The Personnel Ledger drives all Provisioning.** Anything that changes after the ledger has triggered an action has to be redone by hand.
