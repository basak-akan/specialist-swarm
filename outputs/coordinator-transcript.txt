## Summary of the deal position I took

**The specialists all agreed on the shape of the deal; I had one real judgement call to make.**

- **Pricing** recommended 25% headline (floor 28%) vs. Acme's 35% ask, with structured levers (Year-1 ramp, $150K migration credit, deferred ML tier) to give Acme visible value without moving the headline rate. I took this straight.
- **Legal** flagged five walk-aways in §4 plus the §3.5 termination-for-convenience and §3.4 MFN. I used their counter-text verbatim in §4 of the response, including the Stark Industries IP precedent for §4.4.
- **Technical Fit** confirmed "strong fit with one caveat" — 99.99% SLA needs multi-region active-active. **One judgement call here:** Pricing said bake the $100K SLA cost into the base; Tech agreed it's doable. I committed to including it in the base price rather than quoting it as an add-on — this neutralises an evaluation-criteria gotcha and makes our TCO cleanly comparable.
- **Competitive Intel** correctly identified Microsoft Fabric as a political head-fake, not the real technical threat. I used Initech Sensors (same industry, beat Fabric Nov 2025) as the lead reference and positioned the deal around open formats + per-table residency + real-time at scale, not generic differentiation.
